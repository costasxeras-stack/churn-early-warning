A retention team can contact some customers. Who should be on the list?

IBM's telecom sample of customers, some of whom left. A simple rule, a points checklist and two models each rank every customer, and the top of each ranking becomes the contact list.

Set the list size

How many customers the team can contact, as a share of all customers. Each method's list is the top of its ranking.

MethodLeavers on the listList who would leaveRevenue of leavers covered

Leavers on the list: share of all customers who left that the list reaches. List who would leave: share of the list that left. Revenue of leavers covered: their monthly charges, as a share of the monthly charges of all leavers.

Rank by risk, or by revenue at risk?

The model's chance of leaving times the monthly bill puts more valuable customers on the list. Same list size, model scores. Each cell: ranked by risk → ranked by revenue at risk.

Who the list misses

Customers who left, split by whether the model's list of 20% reached them.

The data has contract, services and billing, but no usage, complaints or support contacts. Those are the usual signals for longer-term customers who leave.
