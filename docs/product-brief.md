# Product brief

## Problem

Retention teams can contact only some customers. A churn model gives a score per customer, but the team needs a list of a fixed size, a sense of how many on it would really leave, and a reason to trust a model over a simple rule.

## Users

- **Retention and customer success leads** deciding who to contact and how many.
- **Product and data managers** deciding whether a churn model is worth building.
- **Hiring managers** reading this as an example of how I turn a model into a decision.

## Goals

- Compare a rule, a checklist and two models on the same contact lists.
- Let the reader choose the list size.
- Show the tradeoff between ranking by risk and by revenue at risk.
- Say clearly what the data cannot show.

## Non-goals for 0.1.0

- A save rate or a business case. There is no data on what contacting customers achieves.
- Real customer data.
- Model tuning beyond sensible defaults.

## Success measures

- Every number on the page is reproduced by the scripts from a clean folder.
- A retention lead can pick a list size and a ranking after reading the page: [feedback from a reader, to collect].
