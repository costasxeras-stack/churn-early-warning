# Changelog

All notable changes to this project. Versions follow [docs/versioning.md](docs/versioning.md).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-20

First release.

### Added
- Four ways to rank customers by churn risk on IBM's Telco sample: a contract rule, a 5 flag points checklist, logistic regression and gradient boosting, with models scored by 5-fold cross validation.
- Contact list comparison for any list size from 1% to 50% of customers: leavers reached, share of the list that left, and revenue of leavers covered.
- Ranking by revenue at risk, a profile of leavers the list misses, a check of the model's predicted numbers, and a model without gender, senior citizen or family fields.
- Results page with a list size slider, two tables, a comparison of reached and missed leavers, and a chart of predicted against actual.
- Scripts that reproduce every number from a clean folder.
- Case study, product brief and three decision records.
